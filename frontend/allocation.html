Allocation rules
  1. Students are seated in student-code order (repeatable result).
  2. Halls are ordered by usable seats, largest first, then by name.
  3. Fill: fill hall 1 completely, then hall 2… (fewest halls and proctors). Balance: each hall gets a share proportional to its usable seats.
  4. Inside a hall: row by row from the front (A, B…), left to right (1, 2…). Blocked seats are skipped.
  5. Spacing: only odd columns are used, so nobody sits side by side.
  6. Not enough seats → nothing is saved. The whole plan is saved in one transaction.
  7. Later changes (seat blocked, hall resized, roster or halls changed) are re-checked automatically: published plans move affected students to the next free seat; draft plans are marked needs re-allocation.
